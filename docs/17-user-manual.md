# 17 — User Manual (shopper-facing)

**Status:** current · last reviewed 2026-10-29
This is the guide a customer can follow. It matches the shipped pages; there are no
features described here that do not exist.

---

## 1. Finding products

- **Shop** (`shop.html`) lists everything currently for sale.
- **Search** matches product names and categories.
- **Filters**: category, price range, availability. They combine; "Clear" resets them.
- **Sort**: newest, price low→high, price high→low.
- **Product page**: tap a card to see photos, price, description, material, features,
  stock status and reviews.

Sold-out items remain visible but cannot be added to the bag.

## 2. The bag

- "Add to bag" stores the item on your device; the badge shows the count.
- Open the bag to change quantities or remove items.
- The bag survives a page reload. It is cleared when you complete an order.
- Prices in the bag are indicative; the final price is confirmed at checkout from the
  live catalogue.

## 3. Checking out

1. Open **Checkout**.
2. Sign in or create an account if you have not already — orders are tied to an
   account so you can track and return them.
3. Enter:
   - full name, email, phone
   - delivery address, city, state, landmark (optional), delivery instructions
4. Choose delivery: **standard** or **express** (an extra fee may apply).
5. Choose payment: **Pay on delivery** or **Bank transfer**.
6. Apply a promo code if you have one.
7. Confirm.

You will land on a confirmation page with an **order reference** and a **tracking
link**. Bookmark it or keep the email.

If something fails, your bag is kept and the page explains what to do.

## 4. Paying

| Method | How it works |
|---|---|
| Pay on delivery | Pay the courier in cash or by transfer on arrival |
| Bank transfer | The confirmation page shows the account details; use the order reference as the transfer narration |

The shop does not store card numbers and does not take card payments online.

## 5. Tracking an order

- Open **Track order**.
- Enter the reference (e.g. `FX-…`) and the tracking token from your confirmation.
- You will see the status timeline: placed → confirmed → processing → dispatched →
  delivered (or cancelled).
- The same link works without signing in. Keep the token private — anyone with it can
  see that order's status.

## 6. Your account

The account page is split into sections along the left (along the top on phones):

| Section | What it does |
|---|---|
| Overview | four counters (orders, in progress, delivered, saved addresses) and your three latest orders |
| Delivery addresses | your address book — add or remove the addresses checkout offers you |
| Account settings | the name, email and phone on file, password change and email preferences |
| Help & support | tracking, returns, FAQ and contact links |

My orders, Wishlist and Track an order are links in the same menu. **Admin console**
appears there only for the store's administrator accounts; it is not shown to
customers at all.

| Page | What it does |
|---|---|
| Orders | full history with status and totals |
| Returns | request a return against a delivered order |
| Wishlist | items you saved for later |
| Recently viewed | products you opened recently (stored on your device) |

You can add or remove addresses, and delete your account data from the account page.

## 7. Password help

- **Forgot password** → enter your email → follow the link in the email → set a new
  password.
- Links expire for safety. If yours has expired, request another.
- If the email does not arrive, check spam, then contact the store.

## 8. Google sign-in

The "Continue with Google" button appears on the sign-in and registration pages.
It currently shows *"Google sign-in is not enabled yet"* until the store enables the
provider. Use email and password in the meantime.

## 9. Language

- The globe pill on the right edge opens the language list (79 languages, shown with
  their native names).
- Choosing a language translates the page immediately, without reloading it or
  flashing English first. Your choice is remembered on every page.
- Choosing **English** returns to the original text.
- Translation is automatic and may be imperfect; the English version is the
  authoritative one for prices and policies.

## 10. Light and dark

- The sun/moon button switches theme.
- Your choice is remembered on that device.
- Dark is the default.

## 11. Accessibility

- All controls work with a keyboard; focus is visible.
- The reading-progress ring at the right edge shows how far down the page you are;
  click it to jump back to the top.
- Text is sized to remain readable when the browser is zoomed to 200%.

## 12. Contact and policies

- **Contact** page → message form (we reply by email).
- **FAQ** covers delivery times, sizing and returns.
- Footer links: Privacy, Terms, Cookies, Returns, Refund policy.

## 13. Common questions

**Do I need an account to buy?**
Yes, checkout requires one so the order can be tracked and returned.

**Can I change an order after placing it?**
Contact the store with your reference as soon as possible. Before dispatch it can
usually be changed.

**How long is delivery?**
See the FAQ/Returns pages for the current windows; express delivery is available at
checkout for an extra fee.

**Is my payment safe?**
No card data is collected online. You pay on delivery or by bank transfer.

**The page looks wrong / text is in another language.**
Switch the language pill back to English, or clear the site's cookies and reload.
