Frequently Asked Questions

How do I place an order?

Add products to your cart, go to checkout, enter your delivery details and place the order. You can check out as a guest or sign in.

Can I track my order?

Yes. Every order comes with a secure tracking link. You can also sign in and open My Orders.

Can I return an item?

Yes — items in original condition can be returned within 7 days. See our Refund Policy and start a request on the Returns page.

What payment methods are supported?

Pay on delivery and bank transfer (confirmed by the store). Card payments will be added when our payment provider goes live.

Is my data safe?

Yes — you can only see your own orders and details, and every page is served over HTTPS. See our Privacy Policy.

How do I unsubscribe?

Use the unsubscribe page at any time. Order receipts and delivery updates are always sent.
